# Student Retention Analytics — Macquarie University

## Overview
This was a group project for BUSA8031 – Business Analytics Project. The project analysed student retention, attrition, progression, and academic outcomes using de-identified Macquarie University data from 2020 to 2025.

My main contribution was designing and building Dashboard 2: Institutional Retention & Progress Insights (Executive View) in Qlik Sense.

## Business Problem
Macquarie University needed a clearer way to monitor student retention trends across faculties and cohorts. The project focused on helping executive stakeholders identify patterns in retention, completion, equity student outcomes, and academic performance.

## Dataset
The project used two de-identified university datasets:

- Enrolment Data: 284,818 records
- Retention Data: 52,434 records
- Period covered: 2020–2025
- Datasets merged through a shared key

The dataset is not included because it contains real university data and cannot be shared publicly.

## Dashboard 2 Focus
Dashboard 2 was designed for executive stakeholders who needed a high-level view of institutional retention performance.

It focused on:

- Retention trends over time
- Faculty-level retention differences
- Completion and progression patterns
- Equity student retention performance
- Average WAM movement across years
- Strategic monitoring for university decision-making

## Key Findings
Key findings from Dashboard 2 included:

- Retention improved steadily from 2020 to 2024 before plateauing in 2025
- Business and Science & Engineering outperformed other faculties
- Larger faculties showed stronger retention and completion rates
- Equity student retention improved from approximately 68% to 74%, narrowing the gap with non-equity students
- Average WAM rose consistently before stabilising in 2025
